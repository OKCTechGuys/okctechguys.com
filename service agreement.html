1. Services and authorization

This agreement is between OKC Tech Guys LLC ("we," "us") and the customer named below ("you"). It covers in-home and remote technology services, including computer repair, malware removal, data transfer, device setup, home networking, and TV mounting.

  • Estimate first. We diagnose the problem and quote a price before doing any billable work beyond the service call.

2. Back Up First policy

You are responsible for backing up your data before any service begins. Any repair, malware removal, update, or data transfer carries some risk to files, even when done correctly.

  • Before we arrive: back up photos, documents, email, passwords, and anything else you can't replace.
  • Can't back up? We offer a backup service for an extra fee. Ask for it when you book, or check the box below.
  • If you decline a backup, you accept that any data not backed up may be lost.
  • Hardware that is already failing (clicking drives, water damage, won't power on) is at higher risk. We'll tell you before we touch it.
